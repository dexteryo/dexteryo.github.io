Dexter Design System · v1.0.0 · Reference

Colour

One accent that makes a claim, four semantic families that never lie, and six chart colours used in order. Everything else is a considered neutral.

§ 01

The accent is a claim

The viridian accent (--dds-accent) means verified or real. It marks the real-money edge in diagrams, the ok status, verified title-block stamps, and at most one emphasised word in a title. If a reader ever sees the green and the thing is not verified or real, the system is broken.1

Corollary: there is no second accent. When a page feels like it needs another highlight colour, the content needs editing, not the palette.

1. This is why links are ink-with-underline, not green: an unvisited link has earned no claim. They turn accent only on hover.
§ 02

Semantic families

Fixed meanings, shared by status dots, chips, diagram edges, and the title-block stamp. Each family pairs a strong value with a soft background (--dds-*-bg) for chips.

--dds-okverified · healthy · real money · closed risk
--dds-warnin-review · degraded · manual step
--dds-critstale · failing · blocked-by-design
--dds-infomirror · async · informational

Colour never travels alone. Every semantic colour is paired with a shape or word — a dot with a label, a chip with text, a line style with a legend entry. The page must survive greyscale printing and colour-blind readers unchanged.

§ 03

Data-viz palette

Six categorical colours, used strictly in order — series one is always --dds-v1. Do not skip ahead to a “nicer” colour; order is what keeps two charts on the same page comparable.

--dds-v1 …v6 · green, slate, amber, plum, clay, teal

Charts follow the same restraint as everything else: a faint grid, an area fill only when the area means something, and the emphasised series carried by weight — not by a colour outside the palette.

The neutrals are biased, not default: document paper leans warm (toward the serif's world), the panel ground leans cool (toward the instrument's). Both theme flips happen entirely at token level — components never restyle inside a media query. Text/ground pairs are chosen to hold WCAG AA contrast on their own ground in both themes.

§ 05

Rules

  • Tokens only — a hard-coded hex in a component is a defect.
  • One accent. Semantic colours are not accents; charts are not accents.
  • Green = verified/real, everywhere, always. Never decorative.
  • Chips pair --dds-*-bg with the strong value; never invent new pairings.
  • Series order in charts follows v1…v6; a legend is mandatory whenever more than one series appears.
  • Both themes ship with every output; check the accent works on both grounds.
