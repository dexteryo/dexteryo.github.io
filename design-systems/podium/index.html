01 · The shell · pod-points

Four rules carry every slide.

  • Headlines are claims in sentence form, never labels
  • At most four bullets, twelve words each
  • Nothing below 20px except the footer podium.css · --pod-min
  • The author fixes the theme — it never follows the viewer rule 4
