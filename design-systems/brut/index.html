01

Principles

Honest structure. Every container shows its edges: 3px borders, hard offset shadows with zero blur, radius 0. Nothing floats, nothing fades, nothing pretends. A box is a box, and the shadow is a solid slab of ink — béton brut, raw concrete, for documents.

Yellow is a fill, never a voice. The single accent — acid yellow — marks the one thing that matters in each context: the hero metric, the total row, the verdict strip, the hot path through a diagram. It always carries black text and black borders; it never colours type.

Loudness lives in furniture. Headings run 800–900 weight, uppercase, tight. Body prose stays at weight 400, 17px, capped at 72 characters. A report you cannot read is not brutalist; it is broken.

Systems are drawn, not narrated. If prose walks through a request hop by hop, the hops are drawn as a native SVG diagram: paper-sticker nodes coloured by kind, 3px edges coloured by route, numbered steps keyed to a list. Every hue in a figure is declared in its legend.

Energy without chaos. One spacing scale, one accent, one display face, at most one tilt per page. The colour in diagrams is rationed by meaning — that is what separates a system from a poster.

03

Layout & reading

The page is poured full width. .brut-page spans the viewport with a fluid gutter; inside it, .brut-layout puts a sticky contents rail beside the frame from 1200px and stacks them below that. Prose keeps its 72-character measure; diagrams, tables, service grids and stat strips take the whole width. This page is the layout.

skeleton.htmlhtml
<body class="brut-doc"><div class="brut-page">
  <header class="brut-masthead">…</header>
  <div class="brut-layout">
    <nav class="brut-toc" aria-label="Contents">
      <div class="brut-toc-h">Contents</div>
      <ol><li><a href="#context"><span>01</span>Context</a></li>…</ol>
    </nav>
    <main class="brut-frame"><section class="brut-section" id="context">…</section></main>
  </div>
</div></body>

The rail's active highlight comes from a twelve-line IntersectionObserver at the foot of the page; without script, the rail is a list of plain anchor links and every section is still reachable.

06

Architecture diagrams

Native SVG, hand-authored, one grammar for three figure types. Nodes are paper stickers — acid fill by kind, black 3px outline, hard slab shadow; stores are cylinders and queues carry three bars. Edges are 3px strokes coloured by route; marching dashes point in the direction of travel and square packets ride the paths that matter. State is on the slab: the hot path's slab turns acid, a failing node's turns red, a proposed node has no slab at all because it has not been poured yet.

System context — wallet top-up

  1. A client posts a top-up with an Idempotency-Key header to the API gateway.
  2. The gateway terminates mTLS, checks the JWT against auth-svc, and routes to payments-api.
  3. payments-api claims the key in idem-cache; a repeat key returns the stored response.
  4. payments-api authorises the amount with the card PSP, passing the same key downstream.
  5. On authorisation it credits wallet-svc, which commits to wallet-db.
  6. payments-api publishes TopupAuthorised to the topups topic.
  7. The PSP's capture webhook lands on psp-webhooks, which publishes to the same topic; poison messages go to topups.dlq.
  8. The proposed ledger-svc consumes the topic inside the PCI boundary and appends to ledger-db.
  9. ledger-svc schedules the T+1 payout over the SEPA rail.

Grammar

Build every node, edge and packet from these pieces. Keep the viewBox no wider than about 990 units so a figure renders near 1:1 beside the contents rail and no text drops below 11px; SVG text is never smaller than 12 units. Marker IDs must be unique per page, so prefix them per figure (a-sync, s-sync). Draw in this order: zones, edges, packets, nodes, step markers — packets slide under the nodes they pass through. For diagram shapes the grammar does not cover (Gantt, ER, mind map), pre-render Mermaid with mermaid-config.json into a legacy .brut-diagram slab.

Decision record + trade-off matrix

ADR-014

Post to the ledger from an outbox, not the request path

proposed
Context

payments-api writes the ledger synchronously after the PSP authorises. When the ledger is slow, top-ups time out after money has moved, and clients retry.

Decision

Publish TopupAuthorised through a transactional outbox; a new ledger-service consumes it inside the PCI boundary and appends to ledger-db.

Consequences
  • p95 drops by the ledger write, about 90ms.
  • Ledger failures no longer fail a paid top-up.
  • Ledger is eventually consistent, seconds behind.
  • One more service to run and page for.

Callouts — note · warn · risk · decision

Note

The PSP retains idempotency keys for 24 hours; ours match.

Warning

Do not reuse a key across amounts — the PSP returns the first response.

Risk

During the canary, a ledger outage hides drift for up to an hour.

Decision

Partition topup.events by wallet ID, never by merchant.

Anti-patterns

NeverBecause
GradientsFills are flat; a gradient is a fill that cannot make up its mind
Blur / soft shadowsShadows are hard slabs of ink; blur is a lie about light
Grey-on-greyInk on ground or nothing — low contrast is low honesty
Yellow textThe accent has no contrast to give; it carries black type as a fill
Rounded cardsRadius 0; only the sticker chips round
A centred 900px columnv2 pours full width; only prose keeps a measure
A hue without a legend entryEvery route and node colour in a figure is a declared meaning
Packets on every edgeMotion marks the one to three routes that matter; the rest stand still
ASCII art, screenshots, Mermaid for topologyArchitecture, sequence, and state are drawn in the native grammar
Decoration as hierarchyA fill or a tilt never substitutes for a real heading or a real verdict
