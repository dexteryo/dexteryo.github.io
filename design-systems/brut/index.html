01

Principles

Honest structure. Every container shows its edges: 3px borders, hard offset shadows with zero blur, radius 0. Nothing floats, nothing fades, nothing pretends. A box is a box, and the shadow is a solid slab of ink — béton brut, raw concrete, for documents.

Yellow is a fill, never a voice. The single accent — acid yellow — marks the one thing that matters in each context: the hero metric, the total row, the verdict strip. It always carries black text and black borders; it never colours type, because it has no contrast to give.

Loudness lives in furniture. Headings run 800–900 weight, uppercase, tight. Body prose stays at weight 400, 16px, capped at 70ch. A report you cannot read is not brutalist; it is broken.

Energy without chaos. One spacing scale, one accent, one display face, at most one tilt per page. The noise is rationed — that is what separates a system from a poster.

05

Diagrams

Brut reuses the DDS two-tier diagram discipline and reskins it: the semantic edge and node grammar is unchanged (a restyle may recolour it, never reinterpret it), and every diagram carries a legend plus a title block. Mermaid diagrams pre-render with this directory's mermaid-config.json. The frame is a .brut-diagram slab.

Title block, revision date, and verification status are mandatory on real drawings — see the DDS README §6 for the full grammar and the coverage table. Prose that narrates a flow step by step must also draw it.

06

Anti-patterns

NeverBecause
GradientsFills are flat; a gradient is a fill that cannot make up its mind
Blur / soft shadowsShadows are hard slabs of ink; blur is a lie about light
Grey-on-greyInk on ground or nothing — low contrast is low honesty
Yellow textThe accent has no contrast to give; it carries black type as a fill
Rounded cardsRadius 0; only the sticker chips round
A second display faceHelvetica shouts and talks; mono attests; nobody else speaks
Borders under 1.5pxA 1px hairline belongs to another system
Decoration as hierarchyA fill or a tilt never substitutes for a real heading or a real verdict
