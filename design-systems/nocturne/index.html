01 —

Principles

Borders carry elevation. Hierarchy comes from the surface scale — ground, surface, raised — separated by 1px hairlines. The only shadow in the whole system is the card's one-pixel top inner highlight: machined edge light, not elevation theatre.

One accent outside the figure, a full palette inside it. Restrained indigo marks what is actionable or singular: links, section numbers, the verdict bar, focus rings. Inside a diagram, hue is an encoding — six route colours and eight node kinds, every one declared in the legend. Colour that does not mean something is a defect in both places.

Wide for systems, narrow for sentences. The page spans the screen so architecture, tables, and dashboards get room to breathe; prose stays at a 72-character measure so it is still comfortable to read. A sticky contents rail keeps long design docs navigable.

Motion shows direction, never mood. Dashed edges march the way data flows and packets travel the hot path. Nothing pulses, glows, or bounces, and all of it stops under reduced motion.

Machine truth wears the machine face. Every metric, timestamp, ID, and piece of evidence is monospace with tabular numerals. Claims cite their source in a ref pill FundsRouter.java:214 inline with the claim.

Portability is a hard constraint. System font stacks only, no webfonts, no external requests. Output must survive a sandboxed artifact viewer, a ticket attachment, and a printer.

The comparison job keys on wallet ID and tolerates nothing above a euro:

recon/drift.sqlsql
SELECT w.wallet_id, w.balance - j.mirrored_balance AS gap
FROM   wallets w JOIN journal_rollup j USING (wallet_id)
WHERE  ABS(w.balance - j.mirrored_balance) >= 1.00;  -- euro tolerance, not cents

Reconcile the mirrors nightly, not monthly.

The drift window is the exposure. A nightly three-way check — float, wallet ledger, journal — shrinks the maximum undetected divergence from thirty days to one.

06 —

Architecture diagrams

Hand-authored SVG inside .noct-arch: a dot-grid canvas, nodes tinted by kind, edges coloured by route, zones for VPCs and trust boundaries, numbered steps that key the picture to the prose. Dashes march in the direction of flow; packets ride the one to three routes that matter. Hover a legend entry to isolate a route.

Container view — wallet top-up

  1. Client posts a top-up through the gateway — TLS termination, WAF, and a per-key rate limit.
  2. The gateway verifies the JWT with auth-service before routing anything (control plane).
  3. wallet-service takes the request on the hot path and checks the idempotency key in Redis.
  4. A pending double-entry row lands in ledger-db inside the same transaction as the outbox row.
  5. payments-adapter charges the PSP from inside the PCI scope; card data never leaves it.
  6. On success, wallet.funded is published from the outbox; consumers fan out asynchronously.
  7. notify-worker retries three times, then the message lands in notify.dlq for replay.

Architecture decision record — .noct-adr

ADR-014

Publish wallet.funded through a transactional outbox

accepted 2026-09-18 · team-wallet
Context

wallet-service writes the ledger row and then publishes to SNS. When the publish fails after commit, the wallet is funded but nobody hears about it — 0.3% of top-ups in August INC-088.

Decision

Write the event to an outbox table in the same transaction as the ledger entry. A relay process publishes and marks rows sent; consumers dedupe on event ID.

Consequences
  • Event is exactly as durable as the money movement.
  • Replay is a SQL query, not a script.
  • Adds ~40 ms median publish lag.
  • One more process to run and alarm on.

Callouts — .noct-callout (note · warn · risk · decision)

Note

The relay is single-writer by design; scale it by partitioning on wallet ID, not by adding replicas.

Warning

Outbox rows are retained 7 days. Replays older than that need the ledger export.

Risk

If the relay stalls, consumers see nothing and no alarm fires today. Owner: team-wallet.

Decision

Alert when the oldest unsent outbox row is older than 60 s.

09 —

Anti-patterns

The generic AI dark mode this system exists to prevent:

  • Gradient text, gradient heroes. The hero is type, space, and at most the dot grid.
  • Glassmorphism / backdrop blur. Surfaces are opaque; hairlines separate them.
  • Drop shadows or glow on nodes. A hot node gets a thicker stroke and a deeper tint, never a halo.
  • Rainbow diagrams. A hue without a legend entry is decoration. If two routes mean the same thing, they share a colour.
  • Decorative motion. Packets on every edge, pulsing nodes, bouncing arrows. Motion shows direction on the routes that matter.
  • Full-width prose. 200-character lines are unreadable; the measure stays at 72ch even on a 2560px screen.
  • Thin display type on dark. 550–650 or nothing.
