You chose the agentic-commerce lane on the strength of the September 2025 "Buy it in ChatGPT" launch. Verified research shows that world has already turned once: OpenAI deprioritised native in-chat checkout on 24 March 2026 and pivoted to discovery-first shopping completed on the merchant's own site. The winning weekend product is therefore not checkout middleware — it is the feed, readiness and syndication layer that gets long-tail merchants seen by every shopping agent. This document is the build plan: product, fund flows, go-to-market, and the ChatGPT application playbook.

§ 01

What changed since the launch post you read

The 29 September 2025 launch was real but narrower than its headline: US ChatGPT users could buy from US Etsy sellers at once, while the "over a million" Shopify merchants were only ever "coming soon".1 The developer docs still draw the load-bearing distinction: building with ACP is open to all; Instant Checkout in ChatGPT is available to approved partners, applied for at chatgpt.com/merchants.2

Native in-chat checkout then failed to scale. Forrester reported Shopify itself confirming only about 30 merchants ever went live (Rye's count: roughly 12); Walmart found in-chat checkout converted at about one-third the rate of a click-through to walmart.com.3 On 24 March 2026 OpenAI deprioritised in-chat Instant Checkout — "the initial version … did not offer the level of flexibility that we aspire to provide" — and pivoted to discovery-first shopping (image and budget search, comparison), with purchases completed on the merchant's site or in merchant-built ChatGPT apps. Seven large retailers were live at the pivot: Target, Sephora, Nordstrom, Lowe's, Best Buy, Home Depot, Wayfair.4 The 4% Shopify fee (opt-in, from 26 January 2026) died with it; ChatGPT-referred sales now carry no OpenAI fee.5

The same day, Shopify switched on Agentic Storefronts by default for 5.6 million stores across ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode and Gemini — with checkout completing on the merchant's own site via redirect or in-app browser.6 Meanwhile the protocol itself keeps compounding: five breaking releases in seven months, Meta joining OpenAI and Stripe as lead maintainer, and the 2026-04-17 release adding a push Feed API, Cart API, MCP transport, /.well-known/acp.json discovery and 3DS2 delegated authentication.7 PayPal operates its own ACP server, and the checkout spec's payment token now names Stripe, Adyen and Braintree as providers.8

Reading analysis — the money surface (in-chat checkout) contracted to a handful of giants, but the attention surface exploded and became multi-rail. Every rail — ChatGPT, Google's UCP, Copilot, Perplexity — feeds on the same thing: structured, accurate, agent-readable product data. That is the layer a solo builder can own for the long tail.

§ 02

What we build: one catalog in, every agent surface out

Working name: AgentReady analysis — a feed & readiness platform for long-tail merchants, WooCommerce first. Three components, shipped in order:

  1. Readiness scanner (free, the front door). Paste a store URL → an Agent Readiness Score with a fix list: feed presence and required-field coverage, schema.org Product/Offer structured data, AI-crawler access, policy pages (terms, privacy, returns — Shopify makes these a hard requirement for its ChatGPT channel9), /.well-known/acp.json, image and variant quality. Shareable result page; aggregate data feeds a quarterly index (§ 05).
  2. Feed engine (the paid core). Connect the store (Woo plugin / REST / CSV) → normalise → LLM-enrich titles, descriptions, attributes and variant modelling per OpenAI's published feed best practices → validate → syndicate: ACP feed (daily full file + incremental API upserts, once approved), Google Merchant Center (which UCP, AI Mode and Gemini consume), Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity's SFTP intake, which follows the Google feed spec.10 Continuous monitoring absorbs the spec churn — five breaking ACP releases in seven months is a subscription argument, not a bug.7
  3. Application concierge (top tier / one-off). Assembles the chatgpt.com/merchants package: compliant feed sample, policy checklist, category screening against OpenAI's prohibited list, and — for merchants pursuing full Instant Checkout where it still exists — the sandbox evidence pack OpenAI requires: attestation of compliance, end-to-end test logs, HMAC webhook delivery, idempotency behaviour, TLS and IP allowlisting.11

Non-goals, stated as design: no checkout middleware (the March pivot killed its long-tail case; Firmly, Rye and Bold fight over the enterprise version with claims like 45-minute onboarding versus $250k–500k per-channel builds12); never touch the payment token, card data or merchant funds (delegated-payment integration explicitly drags PCI scope13); never merchant of record. The enterprise validator: Feedonomics launched exactly this category — Agentic Catalog Exports, syndicating to OpenAI, Gemini, Copilot, PayPal, Stripe, Perplexity, Amazon — on 27 April 2026, enterprise-only. The long tail below it is the open flank.14

Why weekend-maintainable analysis — it is a data pipeline plus LLM calls on a schedule. The failure mode is a stale feed, not lost money. WooCommerce first because native support arrived late and thin (an MCP server for product/order management in October 2025; a checkout-capable gateway flag only in WooCommerce 10.7, April 2026) and third-party ACP plugins are negligible — a WordPress.org search for "agentic commerce" returns 38 results, nearly all under 100 active installs. The namespace is still unclaimed.15

§ 03

Fund flows: our money, the merchant's money, and the gated exception

Three flows matter, and only the first involves us at all. Our revenue is plain SaaS subscription money (DWG 03-01). The merchant's sale — the mainstream, post-pivot path — completes on their own site with their own PSP; we exist in it purely as catalog data (DWG 03-02). The gated exception, in-chat checkout for approved partners, still runs on delegated payment tokens; we prepare merchants for it but never hold the token (DWG 03-03). The merchant is merchant of record throughout: the card statement shows the merchant's name, and refunds, chargebacks and sales tax stay with the merchant, not OpenAI.16

§ 04

Market and economics: early, growing, honestly small today

The demand curve is real. Adobe Analytics measured AI-referred US holiday retail traffic up 693.4% year on year (Nov–Dec 2025), converting 31% better than other traffic; by March 2026 AI-driven traffic converted 42% better than non-AI — an 80-point reversal from a year earlier.19 ChatGPT reached ~900 million weekly users with roughly 50 million shopping queries a day.20 But the base is small: Etsy's agentic traffic grew ~15× and still sat under 1% of visits, with marketplace GMS flat.21 This is a position-for-the-curve market, not a harvest market.

Pricing anchors: DataFeedWatch (ad-channel feeds, the closest mature analogue) runs $64–599/month by SKU count22; WooCommerce extensions norm at $99–199/year; Feedonomics and Productsup are enterprise, no public pricing. The dossier's retention data says sub-$50/month AI products churn brutally (23% GRR) — mitigation here: annual-first pricing in the WooCommerce idiom, and a product whose value (feeds keep flowing, specs keep absorbed) is infrastructural rather than novelty.

Proposed tiers analysis — Free (scanner + 1 rail, capped SKUs) · Starter $290/yr (1k SKUs, 2 rails) · Growth $790/yr (10k SKUs, all rails, LLM enrichment, monitoring + alerts) · Agency $1,990/yr (white-label, 10 stores). Monthly available at +25%. Realistic arc at freemium norms (1–2% conversion, top performers ~5%23): 10k scans and 3k plugin installs in year one yields 40–90 paying stores ≈ $2–5k MRR-equivalent — meaningful passive income, not a rocket. The WordPress plugin economy context cuts both ways: 80% of surveyed plugin businesses were flat or declining in 2025 and organic search CTR fell ~57% — but that pain is precisely the "be visible where AI answers" anxiety this product monetises.24

§ 05

Marketing: how merchants find it, trust it, and pay for it

analysis The channel strategy assumes the Barn2 lesson: classic SEO for plugins is dying (−57% organic CTR), so distribution must ride surfaces AI answers cite and directories people still search directly. The ladder (DWG 05-01):

  1. The scanner is the campaign. "Is your store visible to ChatGPT?" is a question every merchant can be made to ask; the score is shareable and specific. Launch on Product Hunt, r/woocommerce, WordPress newsletters (Post Status, WP Tavern pitch), ecommerce X/LinkedIn.
  2. Claim the WordPress.org namespace now. Free plugin under "AI shopping feed / agentic commerce for WooCommerce" — 38 existing results, nearly all under 100 installs, means directory search is winnable land.15 Directory search is unaffected by Google's CTR collapse.
  3. AEO-first content. Maintain the canonical, dated guide — "How to get your WooCommerce store into ChatGPT (updated for ACP 2026-04-17)" — refreshed at every spec release. The goal is being the answer assistants cite, which the spec-churn cadence makes recurring rather than one-off.
  4. The data flywheel. Aggregate scanner results into a quarterly Agentic Readiness Index ("only n% of WooCommerce stores are agent-readable") — the press angle enterprise vendors cannot produce for the long tail.
  5. Agencies as multiplier. White-label readiness reports plus 20–30% referral; agencies are already packaging "get into ChatGPT" services, and the concierge tier is their deliverable.
  6. Marketplaces second. WooCommerce Marketplace (70% revenue share to the developer25) once reviews exist; a Shopify app later — thinner value there since storefronts are native, but monitoring/enrichment still sells.

§ 06

The ChatGPT strategy: apply where the door is, hedge where it isn't

The honest state of the front door: chatgpt.com/merchants remains the application form for feed ingestion and (residual) Instant Checkout, but since the pivot OpenAI has narrowed near-term scope to a small pool of large, integrated retailers.26 The playbook (DWG 06-01) treats the ChatGPT application as one path among four, all served by the same feed asset:

  1. Shopify merchants: nothing to apply for. Agentic Storefronts are on by default; the work is eligibility hygiene (policies complete, B2B-only excluded, sells to US customers — the store itself can be based anywhere) and feed quality.9
  2. Etsy US sellers: ride Offsite Ads — enrollment is automatic; the lever is listing quality.27
  3. WooCommerce / custom carts: prepare, then apply. Preconditions before the form: required-field-complete feed, policy pages live, prohibited-category screen passed, structured data and AI-crawler access verified. Then submit at chatgpt.com/merchants with the concierge pack. Expect gating; treat approval as upside, not the plan.
  4. While gated, ship the open rails — Google Merchant Center (UCP, AI Mode, Gemini; existing feeds work28), Microsoft Copilot (500k US merchants; PayPal/Stripe merchants apply by form; UCP supported since April29), Perplexity (free, Google-spec feed via SFTP10) — plus organic ChatGPT visibility through crawlable structured data, which discovery-first shopping reads even for unapproved merchants. Amazon is the one closed continent: it sued Perplexity's agent, won the injunction, and built its own.30

Vendor-side moves analysis — participate in the ACP repo through the SEP process (visibility among the exact engineers building this economy), contact acp@stripe.com as an ecosystem integrator, and pursue "approved partner" status for feed submission the way Productsup and Feedonomics did — as the integrator of record for many merchants, which is the strongest single argument for the application: OpenAI approves one competent partner and gains a thousand clean long-tail feeds.

§ 07

Risks, watchlist, and what verification killed

analysis Ranked by how much each would hurt:

  1. Platform absorption. WooCommerce 10.7 already ships a native gateway flag; Stripe's merchant-facing "Agents" product will eventually leave private preview.31 Moat answer: quality, multi-rail reach and monitoring — connectivity alone will commoditise.
  2. The ChatGPT gate stays shut to the long tail. Then the product is a Google/Copilot/Perplexity syndicator with ChatGPT organic visibility — still viable, weaker headline. This is why every marketing surface says "every AI shopping surface", not "ChatGPT".
  3. Rail wars. UCP (Google, 20+ endorsers) versus ACP (OpenAI/Stripe/Meta) could fork feed formats further. Mitigation is the product itself: format translation is the job.
  4. Demand timing. Agentic traffic is still <1% of visits at Etsy scale.21 If the curve stalls, revenue plateaus at hobby scale. Watch Adobe's quarterly numbers.
  5. Solo bandwidth. Five spec releases in seven months is absorbable only because feed formats fail soft. Keep the no-checkout rule for exactly this reason.

Refuted in verification — do not repeat as fact: that OpenAI killed Instant Checkout on 4–6 March (correct: deprioritised 24 March, and "discontinued" overstates — it persists for approved partners/apps); that Stripe remains the only delegated-payment PSP (PayPal live since Oct 2025; Adyen/Braintree in the enum); that the 4% Shopify fee is current (died with the pivot).

Employer proximity — unchanged from the dossier: feed and discovery tooling holds no funds, issues no cards, and shares no surface with an EU business-card issuing platform. Distant, with the standard hygiene rules (own hardware, own time, no CleverCards code or confidential context).

Annual pricing in the $290–1,990 band. No checkout, no tokens, no funds — ever. Re-evaluate the checkout question only if OpenAI reopens Instant Checkout to the long tail with a stable spec.
